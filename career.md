# Career Philosophy

> A great book is not one where you have nothing to add, but rather when you can remove nothing anymore.

This repository is my lean, focused toolkit for job hunting. No application-specific details here—just the evergreen assets and preparation materials that serve me across any opportunity.

---

## Core Documents

### CV
A comprehensive record of achievements, roles, and impact. The full story when someone wants depth.

### Brag Document
Updated every 1-2 quarters. Captures what I'm doing while it's fresh—projects shipped, problems solved, recognition received. Feeds the CV and resumes.

### Resumes
Two versions, updated annually:
- **IC Track** — Technical depth, hands-on contributions, architectural decisions
- **Manager Track** — Team leadership, hiring, mentorship, organizational impact

---

## Interview Prep

This repo is for preparation, not learning. Questions, frameworks, and practice materials live here. Actual learning and study notes belong elsewhere.

---

## Principles

1. **Keep it lean** — If it doesn't directly serve job hunting, it doesn't belong here
2. **Separate concerns** — Applications go elsewhere; this is the source of truth for career materials
3. **Stay current** — Quarterly brag doc reviews, annual resume refreshes
4. **Prepare, don't cram** — Consistent maintenance beats last-minute scrambling
